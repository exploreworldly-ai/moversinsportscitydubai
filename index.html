A clear booking process helps you understand what is included and what needs to be arranged before moving day.

  1. 1

    Share your moving details. Provide your pickup and delivery addresses, property type, preferred moving date and approximate inventory. Mention whether you are moving a studio, apartment, house, villa or individual furniture items.

  2. 2

    Provide photos or an inventory. Photos of rooms and bulky items help the team assess the volume of belongings, packing needs and possible access challenges.

  3. 3

    Review your quote. Check the quoted cost, included services, packing arrangements and any additional requirements before confirming your booking. Ask about any item or task that is not clearly covered.

  4. 4

    Confirm building arrangements. Reserve an elevator or loading area and obtain any required approvals from property management. Share relevant access instructions with the moving coordinator.

  5. 5

    Prepare and load your belongings. Depending on the agreed service, the team packs items, wraps furniture, dismantles selected pieces and loads the vehicle.

  6. 6

    Transport and unload. Your belongings are transported to the destination and unloaded according to the agreed moving plan. Make sure the destination address and access arrangements are correct.

  7. 7

    Complete the agreed setup. Furniture can be reassembled and items placed in their designated rooms where these tasks are included in your booking. Check the completed work before the team leaves.

The time required depends on the property size, inventory, access conditions and services booked. Confirm the expected schedule with the company rather than relying on a standard completion time.

The cost of hiring movers and packers in Dubai Sports City depends on the amount of work involved. A small studio move with limited furniture generally requires different resources from a large villa relocation with full packing.
